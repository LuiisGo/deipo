# Packaging generation prompt set

Native built-in `image_gen`, reference-based generation/editing. No API/CLI fallback. The following consolidates the generation instructions for reuse; each frame was requested as an individual image, not as a contact sheet.

## Shared direction and invariants

Create one premium editorial product photograph for deipo., landscape 4:3. Use the owner-supplied packaging concept as the design anchor and the prepared original cream logo as the artwork reference. Preserve the matte-black hinged food mailer, cream deipo. wordmark with small orange period, centered vertical orange security seal and restrained front-corner information. The seal reads DROP 001 / SUNDAY ROAST. Do not invent new labels, customer information, dates or decorative branding.

Warm cream #F5F1E8 seamless editorial background; refined three-quarter slightly top-down camera; controlled soft upper-left studio lighting; subtle shadow; tactile matte paper. Maintain the same box, base orientation, photographic family and scene across the sequence. No people, hands, utensils, props, clutter, glossy plastic, dark void, fake 3D, dramatic steam or text outside the packaging. Keep the complete object and raised lid comfortably inside the main compositions. Tighter seal/interior details are deliberate editorial crops.

## Frame 01 — Closed

Faithfully rephotograph the approved packaging object, fully closed, seal intact. Show the lid, front and modest side perspective. Keep generous air around the box, with room for a future opening lid in the same scene. Preserve the exact wordmark geometry and packaging system. Calm, controlled first moment.

Inputs: approved packaging concept and original cream logo.

## Frame 02 — Seal detail

Edit frame 01. Keep the box fully closed and preserve its materials, typography, seal, angle, background and lighting. Change only the framing to a modest closer crop emphasizing the orange seal and front lid edge. Retain the wordmark and enough box context to identify the same object. Not an extreme macro. Increase anticipation through detail, without adding elements.

Input: generated frame 01.

## Frame 03 — First opening

Edit frame 01 with camera, box base, backdrop and lighting locked. Break the orange seal cleanly at the front lid seam; retain its upper piece on the lid and its printed lower piece on the box front. Lift the hinged lid only 8–12 degrees at the rear hinge. Show a narrow dark crack and a hint of cream interior paper, without revealing the food. No detached/floating lid or changed box construction.

Input: generated frame 01.

## Frame 05 — Full reveal and interior anchor

Fully open the same hinged lid backward, keeping the entire lid in frame. The inside has a small faithful cream deipo. wordmark with orange period. Preserve the box front and broken seal system. Show Sunday Roast in a fitted black tray with cream parchment: rosy sliced roast beef center, golden roast potatoes right, Yorkshire pudding back left, glazed carrots and dark greens left, rich jus sheen. Use the existing food photograph for food only, without its ceramic plate.

A slim cream editorial insert/receipt occupies the dry right interior channel, separate from food, with restrained deipo. / DROP 001 text. No invented operating information. Keep the food appetizing, tactile and physically plausible in the box. Preserve camera family, cream environment and soft light.

Inputs: frames 01 and 03, existing Sunday Roast photograph, original cream logo.

Framing correction to the initial output: preserve all objects, food, typography, light and angle; pull back approximately 14 percent to leave at least 7 percent cream air above the complete upper lid. No other change. The corrected image is the saved final frame 05 and the reference for frames 04 and 06.

## Frame 04 — Half open

Create the intermediate moment between frames 03 and 05. Lift the same hinged lid about 40 degrees, so its outer logo remains visible and the lid still obscures the rear part of the interior. Use exactly the full-reveal arrangement of tray, parchment, food and slim right-side insert. The opening is clearly deeper than frame 03, but not the complete hero reveal. Preserve the broken seal, box identity, background, light and camera family. Entire object remains inside the image with cream air.

Inputs: frame 03 and corrected frame 05.

## Frame 06 — Final ritual

Edit corrected frame 05, retaining the open box, food layout, tray, paper, insert and branding. Change only the editorial framing: a modest closer view shifted toward the right interior channel, emphasizing receipt-style insert, cream paper texture, black rim and inside-lid wordmark. The Sunday Roast remains visible on the left. Do not enlarge the insert, rearrange food or add text. Finish as a calm tactile brand/material still life within the same scene.

Input: corrected frame 05.

## Export

Keep the generated PNG masters. Create same-size WebP exports at quality 86 with no content edits, recoloring, retouching or resampling. Centralized drop content points to the WebPs. Production photography/manufacturing artwork and physical packaging validation remain separate future work.
